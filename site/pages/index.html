All Eyes On Me

The art director of a product coded by agents.

Your agents ship features. AEOM makes the front look like someone cared: type /aeom, it judges every screen, redraws the front on a direction drawn from your product, and opens a pull request.

Star on GitHub Try it

One run

A deliberately ugly shop, and the same shop after one run. Same pages, same buttons, no feature added.

One command

  1. It understands your product. What it is for, who uses it, the journeys that matter.
  2. It judges every screen. At phone and desktop width, against eight principles.
  3. You pick a direction. Three, drawn from your product's own world, never from the looks AIs reach for.
  4. It rebuilds, and keeps what got better. Then a pull request, and the standard your next ones follow.
