# /aeom: directions from a prompt

Part of the `/aeom` skill: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) says when to read this file (`/aeom --depuis <prompt.md>`). From SKILL.md, a run from a prompt needs only **A run that stops, and is taken up again**; the banned looks, the paragraph on the product's name and the screening brief are in [directions.md](directions.md) and [phase-directions.md](phase-directions.md).

## 2d. Directions from a prompt (`/aeom --depuis <prompt.md>`)

There is no app and no visual yet: a prompt, such as the one `/style` writes, and three directions drawn from it in minutes, for the person to choose. Nothing in the project changes: no run branch, no worktree, no worker in the project's code, no product sheet. Everything is written in `.aeom/runs/$RUN/`. Speed is the point: the session reads, three workers draw at once, one judge screens, the person chooses. The person chooses even with `--autonome`: no judge picks a direction for them.

1. **Read the brief**, the session alone. `RUN` = `run-<YYYYMMDD-HHMM>`, then `mkdir -p .aeom/runs/$RUN`. The prompt is the file given, or the text the user pasted after `--depuis`, written to `.aeom/runs/$RUN/prompt.md` so a run taken up still has it. The charter is `.veebee/style.md` when it exists. Note the product's name, what it is for, who uses it and on what, the screens the prompt lists (each with who is on it, what they came to do and what must be visible), what the charter fixes (colours, type, feeling, references) and what it leaves open. `WIDTHS` is `390` when the prompt says a phone only, `1280` when it says a computer only, and `390,1280` when it says both or nothing: never a guess. Mark the run: `aeom run mark .aeom/runs/$RUN --phase directions --step 2d.1 --option "depuis <the prompt's file>" --set "WIDTHS=<it>" --set "MAIN=$(pwd)"`.
2. **The cost, in one line, then go.** `aeom estimate --depuis --widths "$WIDTHS"`, with `--directions <n>` when the user asked for more: show its total line and start, without waiting for a go. It is a few agents.
3. **Two screens** from the prompt: the one a user lands on first, as `index.html`, and the densest, a list, a form or a dashboard, as `<its slug>.html`. A prompt that lists one screen: that one, and the one its user opens next. None: the first screen its words describe, and the one its user works in most.
4. **Three angles**, or as many as `--directions` says. When the charter fixes the look, every direction keeps it whole, its colours, type and feeling, and they differ on what it leaves open: the composition, the density, the materials and the light, how the main action is put forward. Without a charter, draw them from the product's world, as directions.md says, with the prompt as the product brief. Give each a short slug and one sentence saying how it reads the charter, or what it is drawn from. The banned looks apply to what the charter leaves open, never against what it asks for in so many words.
5. **One worker per direction, at once**, each an `aeom-worker` (**Briefing any worker**, [phase-wave.md](phase-wave.md)) on this session's own model, passed explicitly to the Agent tool, with the prompt brief below. No worktree: each writes in its own folder, `$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>/`. Mark each as **A run that stops** says.
6. **Capture and check each one** as soon as it replies, its folder served by AEOM itself:
   `aeom capture --dir "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>" --pages /,/<dense>.html --widths <the widest of $WIDTHS> --out "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>-shots"`, then `aeom sheet --out "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>.png" --columns 2 "/=<slug>-shots/index@<width>.png" "/<dense>.html=<slug>-shots/<dense>-html@<width>.png"` (the capture's `manifest.json` lists the files), and `aeom check --dir "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>" --widths "$WIDTHS" --out "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/<slug>-checks"`. A direction a check fails on is not sent back, there is no time for it: what fails is said next to it. A worker whose pages do not open is left out; say so.
7. **Screen, show, choose.** Lay them out: `aeom sheet --out "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions/sheet.png" --columns 1 --width 1600 <slug>=<its image>... --note "<slug>=<its sentence>"...`. One screening judge, an `aeom-judge`, with the screening brief of phase-directions.md and these changes: there is no front as it is, so it is given the prompt and the charter in place of the product brief, and in place of « less polished, less clear or less current than the front as it is », « less polished or less current than what a good team ships this year »; one more reason to name a direction, « it betrays what the charter fixes »; and a banned look the charter asks for in so many words is not one. Lay out again the ones left, then show that sheet and ask, with the session's question tool when it has one: each direction in its two plain sentences, with what its checks found, and the question, which one, or none. Record it: `aeom tournament start --no-knockout --dir "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions" <the slugs left>`, then `aeom tournament decide --dir "$MAIN/.aeom/runs/$RUN/directions" --choose <slug>`, with `--why "<their words>"` when they gave any. None, or none left after the screening: say why, and draw three new angles from what they said, once.
8. **The end.** Say where the chosen direction is, its folder, whose pages a browser opens as they are, and that the whole mockup on it is the next step, not built yet. Mark `--phase done`.

**Prompt brief.** Give the worker its folder, the two screens (file name, who is on it, what they came to do, what must be visible), the charter and the prompt as they are written, its angle and its sentence, the banned looks and the paragraph on the product's name from directions.md, and the widths. Ask it to:

> Draw this direction as two static pages in plain HTML and CSS, `index.html` and `<dense>.html`, with one shared `style.css`, in your folder and nowhere else. Work fast: two pages, not a design system. No framework, no build, no script the pages need: a browser opens them as files. Fonts from Google Fonts or the system; no picture from the web, draw with CSS or inline SVG. Fill them with believable data in the product's language and words, taken from the prompt (names, amounts, dates), never lorem ipsum. Keep exactly what the charter fixes, its colours, type and feeling; your angle decides only what it leaves open. Each page holds at the widths given: nothing scrolls sideways, a label stays on one line, cards keep one shape, text is cut only with an ellipsis, and what one taps is big enough. Link the two pages to each other as the product would, by their file names (`index.html`, `<dense>.html`), never from `/`, so they also open as files. Before you reply, name the banned look yours is closest to; if one describes it, start again, unless the charter asks for that look in so many words. Reply in ten lines at most: the two files, then two plain sentences for someone who is not a designer, what one sees, and how it reads the charter.
