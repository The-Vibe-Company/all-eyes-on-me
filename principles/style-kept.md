# The charter kept

What the judge asks of each page when a run keeps the app's style (`/aeom --garder-le-style`, or `"style": "keep"` in `.aeom/config.json`). Each judge looks at the page before the run and after it, side by side.

- `charter-kept` — Next to its capture before the run, the page keeps the app's charter: the same colours, the same fonts, the same logo and the same header. Only a colour whose contrast failed may be a little darker or lighter, in the same hue, and a browser default the charter never chose, such as an unstyled blue link, may take one of the charter's own colours. Where pages disagreed before the run, the home page's charter is the one kept, and a page that came to it passes. Layout, spacing, states and wording may change; the look may not.
